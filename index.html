Simple process

How to Buy

Payment is verified manually. Your private ebook link is sent only after confirmation.

1

Choose a book

Open a book card, check the summary and preview, then tap Buy Now.

2

Scan QR / pay via UPI

Pay the exact displayed amount using the UPI ID or QR shown in the checkout modal.

3

Send screenshot on Telegram

Send your payment screenshot. After manual confirmation, the private Google Drive link is sent on Telegram.

Important: Never share payment screenshots containing unrelated private information. Crazy E-Book never publishes a public Drive or PDF download link.
