Simple process

How to Buy

Payment is verified manually. Your private ebook link is sent only after confirmation.

1

Choose a book

Choose a book and use its existing checkout to create your purchase.

2

Pay by UPI

Pay the exact displayed amount using the UPI ID or QR shown in the checkout modal.

3

Wait for confirmation

Payment is checked manually and an administrator confirms the purchase.

4

Open My Library

After approval, open My Library to access the purchased ebook securely.

Important: Never share payment screenshots containing unrelated private information.
